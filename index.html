About

I studied English Literature in Taipei, and the habit that stuck was reading closely before deciding what something means. I still work that way. In my MSc thesis, I expected a customer's first product to shape what they were worth later. The data said it didn't, and working out why was the most useful part. On a consulting project with Accenture Netherlands, the best question I asked was whether we needed AI at all.

Before that, I worked on the Amazon side of an e-commerce business at HourLoop, resolving listing issues across three North American marketplaces. I've studied in Taiwan, the US and the Netherlands.

I finished my MSc in August 2026 and I'm looking for analyst roles in the UK and the Netherlands where the work feeds into product or business decisions. I've been selected for the UK Youth Mobility Scheme, so I don't need visa sponsorship, and I'm available from November 2026.
